Miramar Residences is an exclusive 4-bedroom penthouse in Panjim's most desirable address, offering sweeping views of the Arabian Sea from every level of this premium tower. A rare combination of city convenience and coastal serenity.

## The Penthouse

This 2,400 sq.ft. penthouse occupies the top floor of Miramar Residences, with floor-to-ceiling windows capturing a 270-degree panorama of the sea and the Mandovi river mouth. The open-plan living and dining area flows onto a wraparound terrace, perfect for entertaining. Four generously proportioned bedrooms, each with an en-suite bathroom, complete this ready-to-move residence.

## Amenities

Residents of Miramar Residences enjoy a resort-style pool, a clubhouse for social gatherings, and a dedicated kids play area. The property is surrounded by a landscaped jogging track, while covered parking and round-the-clock security ensure a worry-free lifestyle.

## Neighbourhood

Miramar is Panjim's premium coastal stretch, home to the iconic Miramar Beach and the famous circular promenade. Panjim's commercial district, with its offices, markets, and restaurants, is minutes away, and Goa University's campus is just a short drive. This is living at the centre of everything Panjim has to offer.
