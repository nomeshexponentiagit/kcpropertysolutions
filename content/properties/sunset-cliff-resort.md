Sunset Cliff Resort commands three acres of Anjuna's legendary headland, with 40 rooms and suites stacked up the cliff for panoramic Arabian Sea views. An established, operating brand generating around ₹6 crore in annual turnover, it is the rare Goan resort where the location itself is the headline attraction.

## The Resort

Forty rooms and suites climb the cliff in stepped terraces, each opening to a private balcony or plunge sit-out facing the sea. The cliffside infinity pool appears to pour into the ocean, the fine-dining restaurant and rooftop bar bookend the sunset hour, and a spacious event lawn hosts weddings and parties that routinely draw 300+ guests. The beach club at the base of the cliff offers direct sea access, while the spa, gym, and wellness deck complete the facilities.

## The Experience

Every evening, guests and locals alike gather at Anjuna's iconic cliff sunset point — a stone's throw from the resort — to watch the sun dissolve into the Arabian Sea. Anjuna's legendary Wednesday flea market and its storied electronic music scene bring a constant stream of international travellers, while the resort's premium hospitality, seasonal events, and established brand name deliver consistently high occupancy from October through May. This is a trophy asset: a proven income stream attached to one of India's most famous coastal locations.

## Location

Anjuna is the epicentre of Goa's counterculture history and, today, of its premium hospitality market. The resort is minutes from Vagator, Chapora, and the cliffs of Ozran, a short drive from the north's most-loved beaches, and around 45 minutes from the new Mopa International Airport. Few properties in Goa can match the combination of revenue, brand equity, and location that Sunset Cliff Resort offers.
