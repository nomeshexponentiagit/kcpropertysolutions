Sand & Sea Inn is a charming 12-room inn on the beach road at Colva, South Goa's most beloved beach village. With friendly hospitality, a loyal repeat clientele, and an easy-going beachside setting, the inn is a warm and dependable hospitality business.

## The Inn

Built across 5,500 sq.ft., the inn offers 12 comfortable rooms with attached baths, a multi-cuisine restaurant, a lush garden, parking for 8 cars, and staff quarters. Direct beach access puts guests just steps away from Colva's white-sand stretch, while the garden and restaurant create a relaxed, holiday atmosphere that keeps guests coming back.

## The Business

The inn runs on the strength of its regulars — guests who return season after season and refer the inn to friends and family. With an annual turnover of ₹1.8 crore and a low-key, efficient operating model, Sand & Sea is ideal for a first-time hospitality buyer or an operator looking to add a steady South Goa asset. The property is available for both outright sale and long-term lease.

## Location

Colva is the crown of South Goa's laid-back tourism — a long, gentle beach lined with shacks, cafes, and warm evening bustle, without the pace of the north. The inn sits right on the beach road, minutes from Colva Beach and well-connected to Margao, making it both a holiday destination and a convenient base.
