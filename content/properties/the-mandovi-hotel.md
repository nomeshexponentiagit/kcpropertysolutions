The Mandovi Hotel is a fully operational 24-room hotel in the heart of Panjim, North Goa's capital city. A landmark address on the banks of the Mandovi River, this turnkey hospitality business offers immediate revenue backed by an established corporate clientele.

## The Hotel

Spread across 12,000 sq.ft., The Mandovi Hotel features 24 well-appointed rooms with attached bathrooms, a full-service restaurant and bar, a conference hall, and a swimming pool. An elevator serves all floors, and the property includes parking for 20 cars and full generator backup, ensuring uninterrupted operations for guests and business functions alike. A dedicated staff of 30 keeps the hotel running like clockwork.

## Operations & Revenue

The hotel enjoys consistent occupancy through the year, driven by a loyal mix of corporate travellers and business delegates. With an annual turnover of ₹3.5 crore and all 24 rooms operational, the property is a proven income generator. Whether for the discerning investor or an operating hotelier, The Mandovi offers a rare city-centre asset in one of Goa's busiest business districts.

## Location

Located on the Mandovi Riverfront, the hotel sits at the centre of Panjim's business district, minutes from the casino cruise berths and the Fontainhas heritage quarter. The casino tourism that defines Panjim's evenings keeps the hotel's restaurant and rooms filled with a steady stream of guests year-round, making this one of the strongest hospitality locations in North Goa.
