Sea Breeze Apartment is a bright 3-bedroom residence in the heart of Dona Paula, one of North Goa's most scenic coastal neighbourhoods. Every room opens to the Arabian Sea, and the famous Dona Paula viewpoint is just minutes from your doorstep.

## The Apartment

Spread across 1,800 sq.ft. of thoughtfully planned space, this 3 BHK apartment is semi-furnished and move-in ready. The sea-facing living room is bathed in natural light, with large windows framing the Dona Paula bay. Three spacious bedrooms each come with attached bathrooms, and the master suite features a private balcony with uninterrupted sea views.

## The Building

The apartment sits within a well-managed gated community with a swimming pool, a fully equipped gym, and covered parking for two cars. With 24/7 security and full power backup, residents enjoy both comfort and peace of mind. The building's elevated position captures cooling sea breezes all year round.

## Location

Dona Paula is famous for its viewpoint overlooking the confluence of the Mandovi and Zuari rivers, a short stroll from the apartment. Miramar Beach is 5 minutes away, while Panjim, the capital city, is a 15-minute drive. Everything from fine dining to water sports is within easy reach.
