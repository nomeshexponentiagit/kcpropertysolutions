Rivera Retreat is a peaceful lakeside guest house in Benaulim, one of South Goa's most tranquil beach villages. Overlooking the calm river, this retreat offers four lake-facing rooms and a restaurant setup in a quiet, leafy neighbourhood.

## The Property

Built over 2,100 sq.ft., Rivera Retreat comprises 4 spacious lake-facing rooms, each with an en-suite bathroom and views of the water. A restaurant setup with a full kitchen is ready for a chef, and the landscaped garden provides the perfect outdoor dining space. The property includes parking for 4 cars and a generator backup, ensuring uninterrupted comfort.

## The Experience

Waking up to the gentle river and birdsong is the everyday experience at Rivera Retreat. The quiet neighbourhood, private garden, and serene waterfront make it ideal for yoga retreats, wellness stays, and slow-travel guests. South Goa's peaceful vibe — a world away from the bustle of the north — is exactly what an increasing number of travellers are seeking.

## Location

Benaulim sits on South Goa's serene coastline, with Benaulim beach just a short drive away. The village is known for its clean sands, quiet coves, and laid-back beach shacks. Margao is 20 minutes away and Dabolim Airport about 40 minutes, keeping the retreat secluded yet well connected.
