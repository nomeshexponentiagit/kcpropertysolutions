Heritage House is a lovingly restored Portuguese colonial mansion in the heart of Fontainhas, Panjim's historic Latin Quarter. With its original azulejo tiles, teak beams, and period detailing, this 4,500 sq.ft. residence offers a rare chance to own a piece of Goa's living history.

## The Residence

The mansion unfolds across two storeys with five bedrooms, a private library, and six bathrooms. High ceilings and a cool stone courtyard temper the tropical heat, while the traditional balcao — the shaded veranda that fronts every Goan home — opens onto the cobbled lanes of Fontainhas.

## Heritage Features

Heritage House retains its original azulejo tile panels, laterite walls, and carved teak joinery. Every restoration detail honours the Portuguese colonial architecture of the 18th century, and the property holds formal heritage status that protects its character for generations to come.

## Location

Situated in the UNESCO-listed Fontainhas heritage zone, Heritage House is moments from Panjim's city centre — the church square, the riverside promenade, and the famous cafes of the Latin Quarter are all within easy walking distance.
