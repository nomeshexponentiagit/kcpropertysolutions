Riverside Land Parcel is a serene 4,200 sq.ft. residential plot set along the calm banks of the Chapora river in Aldona, one of North Goa's greenest and most charming villages. This east-facing parcel offers river views, village quiet, and a short drive to all of Mapusa's conveniences.

## The Property

The plot is flat, cleared, and ready for construction, with a clear title deed and approved residential zoning. Road access, water connection, and electricity at the site make it genuinely ready-to-build, while a short stroll to the river's edge offers daily escapes into Aldona's famous greenery. An east-facing orientation catches the morning sun, making it ideal for tropical architecture that opens onto the river breeze.

## Location

Aldona is nestled in a scenic loop of the Chapora river and is widely regarded as one of Goa's most picturesque inland villages. It is just 10 minutes from Mapusa, North Goa's principal market town, with its banks, hospitals, and schools, and under 30 minutes from the Anjuna–Vagator beach belt. The new Mopa airport is roughly 45 minutes away via the airport highway.

## Why Invest

Riverfront land in Aldona has become increasingly scarce as buyers look beyond the crowded beach belt. The village's idyllic setting has made it a favourite for boutique homestays and private villas, and with North Goa's infrastructure improving year on year, this plot offers strong appreciation potential for both the end-user and the long-term investor.
