Panjim Office Space is a modern 2,200 sq.ft. IT-ready workspace in the heart of Goa's capital city. With an open plan layout, two meeting rooms, and dedicated parking, it is the ideal headquarters for startups, consultancies, and branch offices looking to establish a presence in central Panjim.

## The Office

The office is built for productivity: a flexible open plan floor with abundant natural light, two glass-walled meeting rooms, a pantry, and four dedicated parking slots. The space is semi-furnished with workstations in place, fully air-conditioned, and backed by power backup so business never stops. IT-ready cabling and ample data points mean teams can plug in and go live without major fit-out work.

## Infrastructure

The building offers modern amenities that support serious businesses — reliable power with generator backup, high-speed internet connectivity, secure entry, and professional common areas. The four reserved parking slots are a rare luxury in central Panjim, and the ₹1.5 Lakh/month rent option makes this an attractive occupied investment with immediate income potential.

## Location

Panjim is Goa's capital and its central business district, home to government offices, banks, corporate houses, and a fast-growing IT and startup scene. The office is central to everything: the Secretariat, airport connectivity, and the city's cafés and co-working culture that make talent recruitment easy. With the Mandovi riverfront minutes away and the old city's charm at its doorstep, this is where Goa's new economy is being built.
