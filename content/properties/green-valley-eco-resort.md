Green Valley Eco Resort is a 5-acre jungle sanctuary in Netravali, at the heart of South Goa's eco-tourism corridor. Eighteen handcrafted eco-cottages sit among towering trees and seasonal streams, offering guests an immersive forest experience without sacrificing comfort.

## The Eco Resort

Eighteen cottages are built from locally sourced laterite stone, bamboo, and recycled timber, with thatched roofs and open-plan verandas that dissolve the boundary between room and rainforest. The open-air restaurant serves produce picked that morning from the on-site organic farm, while the spring-fed natural pool replaces the conventional swimming pool. A camping zone, workshop spaces, and a network of marked nature trails round out a property designed for retreats, residencies, and slow travellers alike.

## Sustainable Living

Green Valley operates on a genuinely carbon-neutral footing: solar power covers the resort's energy needs, rainwater is harvested for irrigation, greywater is treated and reused on the farm, and single-use plastics are banned across the property. The organic farm supplies most of the kitchen's vegetables and herbs, and the resort actively partners with local conservation groups to monitor the surrounding forest. It is an operating, profitable business with a clear environmental mandate — rare in the Goan hospitality market.

## Location

The resort sits at the edge of the Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary, home to waterfalls, butterfly gardens, and dense tropical forest teeming with birdlife. Dudhsagar Falls, one of India's tallest waterfalls, is roughly an hour's drive away, and the uncommercialised beaches of Palolem and Agonda are within easy reach. This is South Goa's eco-tourism heartland — quiet, green, and increasingly sought after by travellers looking beyond the coast.
