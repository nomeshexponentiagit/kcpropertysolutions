Coconut Grove Villa is a contemporary 3-bedroom retreat set in the leafy riverfront village of Morjim, one of North Goa's most sought-after beach destinations. Completed in 2025, this tropical modern residence blends clean geometric lines with natural materials for effortless coastal living.

## The Property

Spread across 2,800 sq.ft., Coconut Grove Villa features an open-plan living room, a modular kitchen, and a dedicated office wing. Floor-to-ceiling glazing, locally sourced laterite stone, and teak wood detailing define its tropical modern architecture, while arched windows soften the contemporary silhouette.

## Outdoor Living

The private pool is framed by a palm-shaded garden, with a covered deck perfect for lazy afternoons. Morjim Beach is just five minutes away, where olive ridley turtles nest on the sands each winter — and the village's easy-going charm draws a lively Russian expat community.

## Location

Morjim is a relaxed riverside neighbourhood known for its beach shacks, organic cafes, and low-key luxury. Coconut Grove Villa places you minutes from the beach, with Vagator and Anjuna a short drive north and Panjim about 40 minutes to the south.
