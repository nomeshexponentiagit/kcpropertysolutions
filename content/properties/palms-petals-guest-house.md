Palms & Petals is a well-established guest house in the heart of Calangute, Goa's most happening beach town. With 5 bright en-suite rooms, a charming garden cafe, and consistent walk-in demand, this is a turnkey hospitality business with a proven track record.

## The Property

Spread over 1,800 sq.ft., Palms & Petals features 5 airy en-suite rooms with contemporary furnishings and generous natural light. The ground floor houses the signature garden cafe, a popular breakfast spot for both guests and locals. The property is fully furnished and includes solar water heating, a laundry setup, and parking for 2 cars, with WiFi-ready cabling throughout.

## Business Potential

Calangute attracts the highest tourist footfall in Goa, with visitors streaming in throughout the year. As a running business with an established name and repeat guests, Palms & Petals offers immediate income from day one. The property is available for both outright purchase and long-term lease, making it an ideal entry point for hospitality entrepreneurs.

## Location

Just a short walk from Calangute beach, the property sits on a lively stretch lined with cafes, shops, and nightlife. Baga and Candolim beaches are minutes away, while Mapusa market and Panaji are easily accessible. Dabolim Airport is roughly 45 minutes by road.
