Sunset Bay Villa is a stunning 4-bedroom luxury residence nestled in the serene village of Assagao, North Goa's most coveted address. Designed with Portuguese-Goan architectural elegance, this villa combines old-world charm with modern luxury.

## The Residence

Spread across 3,200 sq.ft. of thoughtfully designed living space, every corner of Sunset Bay Villa tells a story of craftsmanship. High ceilings, arched doorways, and laterite stone accents pay homage to Goa's rich heritage, while floor-to-ceiling windows flood the interiors with natural light.

## Outdoor Living

The private infinity pool overlooks a lush tropical garden, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor living experience. The wraparound veranda is perfect for alfresco dining, and the rooftop terrace offers panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.

## Location

Assagao is one of North Goa's most prestigious villages, known for its boutique cafes, art galleries, and wellness retreats. Sunset Bay Villa is minutes away from Vagator and Anjuna beaches, yet offers the peace and privacy of village life.
