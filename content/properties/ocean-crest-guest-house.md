Ocean Crest is a thriving guest house business on the Candolim beach road. With 6 well-appointed en-suite rooms, a restaurant setup, and direct beach access, this property offers both immediate income and long-term appreciation.

## The Property

Built over three floors, Ocean Crest features 6 spacious guest rooms, each with private balcony and en-suite bathroom. The ground floor houses a fully-equipped commercial kitchen and a 40-seat restaurant space. A rooftop terrace offers panoramic sea views.

## Business Potential

The guest house enjoys high occupancy year-round due to its prime Candolim location. With Goa's tourism sector growing steadily, this property presents an excellent opportunity for hospitality entrepreneurs. The property is available for both outright purchase and long-term lease.
