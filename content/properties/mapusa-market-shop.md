Mapusa Market Shop is a ready-to-occupy ground-floor retail unit on the main market road of Mapusa, North Goa's historic commercial hub. With 800 sq.ft. of prime selling space and a storage mezzanine, it is a rare opportunity to own a shop in one of Goa's most established trading districts.

## The Shop

The shop offers a wide frontage with a rolling shutter and 24/7 market access, making it perfect for grocery, electronics, clothing, or any fast-moving retail business. Inside, the storage mezzanine doubles the usable space without sacrificing the open shop floor, and a dedicated washroom adds convenience for staff and customers. The unit is ready to occupy, so a new owner can open for business within days of purchase.

## Business Potential

Mapusa has anchored North Goa's trade for generations, and its main market road sees consistent footfall from local residents, traders, and tourists alike. The famous Friday Market brings thousands of visitors to the area each week, creating surges of buying activity that benefit every shop on this stretch. At ₹1.2 Cr with a negotiable price, this shop offers one of the most affordable entry points into commercial ownership in North Goa.

## Location

Mapusa is the commercial heart of North Goa, connecting the beach belt of Anjuna, Vagator, and Calangute with the interior towns. The Friday Market is a legendary weekly bazaar drawing crowds from across the state, while the town's bus stand and arterial roads ensure trade flows through the market all day. Shops here benefit from decades of established trading reputation that no new development can replicate.
