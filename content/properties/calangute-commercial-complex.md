Calangute Commercial Complex is a prime mixed-use development in the heart of Goa's busiest tourist belt. Spanning 4,500 sq.ft. across ground plus two upper floors, this property combines high-street retail presence with flexible office space, backed by a proven rental track record.

## The Property

Spread across three levels with a dedicated lift and basement parking, the complex offers divisible units that suit everything from branded retail outlets and cafés to service offices. The ground floor features wide glass storefronts facing the main Calangute road, while the upper floors enjoy generous natural light and views over the bustling beach town. Generator backup and a full fire safety system make the complex secure for long-term tenants.

## Investment Highlights

With a strong existing tenant mix and an 8% rental yield, Calangute Commercial Complex is one of the few income-generating commercial assets available in North Goa's prime belt. The property benefits from year-round tourist and resident footfall, giving it exceptional occupancy resilience and steady appreciation. It is ideal for investors seeking both rental income and capital growth, as well as operators wanting a ready high-visibility location.

## Location

Calangute sits at the centre of the famous Calangute-Candolim belt, Goa's highest-footfall tourist area and its most commercially dense corridor. Restaurants, resorts, travel operators, and retail brands line this stretch, drawing footfall through every season. The complex is minutes from Calangute Beach, well connected to Candolim, Baga, and Panjim, and easily reached by the main highway.
