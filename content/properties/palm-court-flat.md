Palm Court Flat is a neat and practical 2-bedroom home in the heart of Mapusa, North Goa's bustling town centre. Perfect for a small family, this well-maintained apartment offers convenience, comfort, and great value in a thriving community.

## The Flat

This 950 sq.ft. apartment features a smart, compact layout that makes the most of every square foot. The living room opens onto a private balcony, ideal for morning tea and evening breezes. Two comfortable bedrooms come with attached bathrooms, and the modular kitchen is designed for easy everyday living. The flat is available on both sale and rental terms, with the price negotiable for serious buyers.

## The Society

Palm Court is a well-maintained society with a working lift, covered parking, and 24/7 security. Full power backup ensures uninterrupted living, while the communal grounds are kept neat by a dedicated maintenance team. It is a quiet, family-friendly community in one of North Goa's most practical locations.

## Location

Mapusa Market, famous for its vibrant weekly bazaar and fresh produce, is just minutes away, along with supermarkets, banks, and eateries. Top schools and a district hospital are close by, and the town's excellent connectivity links you to Panjim in 20 minutes and the beaches of North Goa in 15. Everything a family needs is within arm's reach.
