Palm Grove Plot is a premium residential plot in the charming village of Siolim, one of North Goa's most desirable locations. This 5,500 sq.ft. corner plot offers the perfect canvas for your dream home.

## The Plot

Located on a quiet village road lined with coconut palms, this rectangular plot is flat, cleared, and ready for construction. The land comes with clear title deed, approved conversion, and all basic utilities at the site boundary.

## Location Advantages

Siolim has emerged as a hotspot for luxury residences, thanks to its proximity to both the beaches and the Chapora River backwaters. The plot is a 5-minute drive from the new Mopa Airport highway and 15 minutes from Morjim and Ashwem beaches.

## Investment Potential

With infrastructure development accelerating in North Goa, plots in established villages like Siolim offer excellent appreciation potential. This is an ideal opportunity for both end-users planning to build a holiday home and investors seeking long-term capital growth.
