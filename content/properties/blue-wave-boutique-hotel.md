Blue Wave Boutique Hotel is a designer 16-room boutique property perched on the cliffs of Vagator, one of North Goa's most sought-after tourism belts. Built around eco-conscious living, it offers panoramic ocean views, Chapora Fort vistas, and a design-forward hospitality experience.

## The Hotel

Spread over 8,000 sq.ft., Blue Wave features 16 designer rooms, a rooftop restaurant with uninterrupted sea views, a swimming pool, and a spa. Parking for 10 cars, solar power, and rainwater harvesting make the property self-sufficient and cost-efficient to run. Every room has been styled with contemporary flair — a stay here is an experience in itself.

## Design & Sustainability

Sustainability is at the heart of the property's design. Solar panels offset a significant share of the energy load, while rainwater harvesting feeds the landscape and pool systems. The result is a boutique hotel with lower operating costs and a story guests love to hear — a growing advantage in upscale North Goa tourism.

## Location

Perched on the Vagator cliffs, the hotel commands sweeping views of Ozran Beach and the historic Chapora Fort. Ten minutes from Vagator Beach and close to Anjuna, this is the heart of North Goa's upscale tourism circuit — sunset parties, beach clubs, and fort-top views just beyond the doorstep.
