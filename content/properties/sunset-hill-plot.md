Sunset Hill Plot is a rare 8,000 sq.ft. hilltop parcel in Anjuna with sweeping sea views and some of the most celebrated sunsets on Goa's coast. Elevated, private, and ready to build, this is a once-in-a-lifetime canvas for a dream villa.

## The Property

The parcel sits on a flat, cleared platform on Anjuna's elevated laterite plateau, with a compound wall already in place. Road access, water connection, and electricity at the site mean construction can begin immediately. The elevated position opens uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea to the west — a front-row seat to Anjuna's legendary sunsets from your own plot.

## Location

Anjuna needs little introduction: its famous Wednesday flea market, cliff-top cafes, and the iconic Anjuna beach are a 5-minute drive away. Vagator, Chapora fort, and Ozran beach are equally close, while Mapusa is about 15 minutes inland. Mopa airport, Goa's newest international gateway, is roughly 40 minutes away.

## Why Invest

Hilltop plots with sea views are among the rarest offerings in North Goa, and Anjuna remains one of its most enduringly popular addresses. Land here has appreciated steadily as boutique hospitality and holiday homes compete for scarce elevated ground. For villa buyers and investors alike, Sunset Hill Plot pairs lifestyle appeal with strong long-term value.
