Coconut Lagoon Resort is a luxurious 32-cottage beachfront retreat spread over 2.5 acres of pristine North Goan shoreline at Ashwem. Operational since 2018, the resort has built a loyal following around its award-winning design, barefoot-luxury ethos, and uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea.

## The Resort

Thirty-two private cottages nestle between the coconut groves and the ocean, each featuring thatched roofs, private sit-outs, and handcrafted interiors of teak, laterite, and local artwork. The property is anchored by a sweeping infinity pool overlooking the sea, two restaurants — one overwater, one garden-side — and a helipad for guests arriving in style. Every cottage is positioned to capture the sea breeze, and no two buildings share the same view.

## Lifestyle & Wellness

Coconut Lagoon is built around slow, restorative living. Mornings begin with sunrise yoga on the dedicated deck, followed by Ayurvedic treatments at the spa that sources oils from local Kerala and Goan traditions. Ashwem's golden beach is a two-minute walk through the property's private access, while nearby Morjim beach is a protected nesting ground for olive ridley turtles — a quiet, conservation-minded stretch that reflects the area's luxury-bohemian character. The resort regularly hosts wellness retreats, live sunset music, and farm-to-table dinners, and enjoys strong repeat occupancy through the year.

## Location

Ashwem is one of North Goa's most beautiful and least commercialised beaches — soft white sand, calm water, and a laid-back vibe favoured by discerning travellers. The resort sits minutes from Morjim's turtle-nesting beaches, 15 minutes from Vagator and Anjuna's cliff sunset points, and roughly an hour from Dabolim International Airport, with the new Mopa airport under 45 minutes away.
