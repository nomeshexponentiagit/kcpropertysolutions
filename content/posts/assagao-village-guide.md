Once a sleepy village known only to locals, Assagao has transformed into North Goa's most desirable address. But what makes this village so special, and is it worth the premium?

## The Village Vibe

Assagao retains its Goan soul despite the influx of luxury properties. Whitewashed churches, colourful Portuguese-era houses, and the smell of freshly baked poi bread from the local bakery — this is Goa at its most authentic.

## The Food Scene

From artisanal cafes like Villa Blanche Bistro to fine dining at Gunpowder, Assagao's food scene rivals any metro city. The village is also home to organic farmers' markets and wellness retreats.

## Property Landscape

Assagao offers everything from restored Portuguese villas to ultra-modern luxury residences. Plot sizes are generous (typically 500-1,000 sq.m.), and the village's elevation offers hilltop properties with sea views.

## Investment Outlook

With limited supply and growing demand from HNIs across India, Assagao properties have seen 30-40% appreciation over the last three years. The opening of Mopa Airport has only accelerated this trend. For those who can afford the premium, Assagao remains North Goa's blue-chip real estate bet.
